How the anonymity estimate works (declared model)

The satisfaction rate of each policy's predicate program is measured against a modeled population of 20,000 synthetic agent credentials — auditScore ~ clipped Normal(78, 12); jurisdiction drawn from a 12-country weighted distribution; capabilities uniform over 3 bits; validUntil uniform over the next 2 years — then multiplied by the real anchored population read from the CredentialAnchor tree of the policy's issuer. It is a k-anonymity-style estimate, like a privacy calculator, not a measurement of real agents. The program evaluation itself is exact: the same postfix semantics the ZK circuit enforces (REF/AND/OR/NOT over LE/GE/EQ triples).
